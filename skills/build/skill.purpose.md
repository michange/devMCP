# @purpose — define the deliverable

Derive one observable purpose from `@preRead`. Name the behavior, boundary, and
result that the cycle will change.

Present:

- the deliverable name;
- its one-sentence purpose;
- its concrete outputs;
- its dependencies;
- its explicit exclusions.

Separate accepted contract from open decisions. Do not expand the active todo.

Once the deliverable is accepted, rename the briefing `<name>.briefing.archived.md`, in place. It is
fresh until this point and obsolete after it, so nothing reads it again. Rename it only when every
one of its points has received its destination at `@preRead`.
